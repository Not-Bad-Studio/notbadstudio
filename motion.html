Photo to Motion

Your professional photos in motion.

Short motion clips made from the photography you already have. For boats, holiday lets, hotels and property, when filming isn't on the cards.

Get a free clip See how it works

One photo, two ways. The simplest thing we do is a slow camera move. Most clips also bring water, sky and light to life.

Why we built this

Good photos shouldn't sit still

Most of the businesses we work with are small: boat yards, holiday lets, independent hotels. They've paid for good photography. Then every platform starts asking for video, and there's no budget, no crew or no calm day to film it.

So we built a way to make the photos they already have work harder. It's the same eye we bring to brand and campaign work, pointed at one job: turning a still into a few seconds people actually watch.

For when filming isn't an option

Boats & charters

The boat's out on charter all season, and filming on water needs a crew, a calm day and luck. Your photos are already there.

Holiday lets

Twenty properties, one photographer's visit each. Filming every one of them doesn't add up.

Hotels & glamping

You can't film summer in February. Your summer photos can still sell next season.

Property

Listings move fast. We move the camera and nothing else, so the home looks exactly as it is.

Not a replacement for filming

If you can film, film. This is for when you can't: the budget's gone on the shoot, the logistics don't work, or you've got great photography that could be doing more.

Built on your photographer's work

How it works

  1. 1

    Send us your website

    Or a link to a folder of photos. There's nothing to prepare.

  2. 2

    Get a free clip

    We pick your strongest shots and make a 10 to 15 second clip. No cost, no commitment.

  3. 3

    Like it? We do the rest

    A fixed price per clip, agreed before we start. Vertical for Reels and TikTok, landscape for your website.

What we will and won't do

  • We only move what's in the photo: the camera, water, sky and light.
  • We never add rooms, views, furniture or people.
  • A person checks every clip before you see it.
  • Clips are labelled as animated from photography, so nobody's misled.

Questions

What do you need from me?

Your website address. If your best photos aren't on it, a link to a folder works too.

Is it AI?

AI tools do the animating. A person picks the photos, directs each move and checks every clip before it reaches you.

Where can I use the clips?

Your website, social, listings and ads. We deliver vertical, square and landscape versions.

What does it cost?

Your first clip is free. After that it's a fixed price per clip, agreed before we start. No retainers.

How long does it take?

Usually a few days for your free clip.
