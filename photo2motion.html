Talk to us

Why not just use an app?

There are plenty of apps that will animate a photo. Here’s what you get from us that you don’t get from them.

Why don’t I just use AI?

You can, and for a single quick post it might do. But image generators add things that were never in your photo, from extra lights and objects to scenery past its edge. And they charge for every clip, including the takes that go wrong, so films for every room, boat or listing soon add up in credits and hours. We pick the photos, direct each shot, check every frame against your photo and deliver one film of one boat, property or room, with your brand on it.

What goes wrong when you do it yourself?

The tools add things. In our own testing, raw output switched on lights that weren’t in the room, put a pen on a bed, invented scenery beyond the edge of the photo, blurred sail numbers and had a guest lift her magazine. One of those in front of a customer undoes the trust your photos built. We catch them before you see anything.

How do you make sure nothing is added?

We check every shot frame by frame against the original photo. If anything appears, changes or moves that shouldn’t, we redo the shot or hold that part of the frame to the photo. Names, numbers and logos stay sharp because we end each shot before they start to drift.

What about people in my photos?

We never animate people. We choose photos without them, and if someone is in frame we hold them still, so they only move with the boat or the camera. Silhouettes are fine.

Will it look like every other AI video?

No. Apps tend to put big zooms and swoops on every photo. We keep the motion subtle, the way a film crew would shoot it: a slow drift, water moving, light shifting across a room. It should look shot, not generated.

Is it made with AI?

The motion comes from the same kind of tools the apps use. What you’re paying for is everything around them: choosing the photos, directing each move, checking every frame, fixing what goes wrong and delivering a finished film in 24 hours.

When will it arrive?

The 24 hours start once we have your order and photos we can use. Order on a Friday afternoon or at the weekend and we start Monday at 9am, UK time. If a photo won’t work, say someone’s in shot or it’s too dark, we’ll tell you within a few hours and start again when the new one arrives. Packs come in daily batches: the first 5 films within 24 hours, then 5 a day. If anything will take longer, we’ll tell you straight away.
