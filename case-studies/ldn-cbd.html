SERVICE

Design / Full-service Marketing / Packaging

CLIENT

LDN CBD

Not Bad Studio had the privilege of partnering with one of our favourite clients for eight transformative years, serving as their full-service creative marketing agency. Together, we delivered a comprehensive suite of services, including social media strategy, SEO, email marketing, paid campaigns, high-level and performance marketing strategies, web development, and analytics. Our work was rooted in a deep understanding of their brand and a shared commitment to making a meaningful impact.  

Beyond their core product line, we also collaborated on branding for three additional product lines within their portfolio, ensuring a cohesive and elevated identity across their offerings. As part of their evolution, we helped them transition to more sustainable packaging solutions, which included the creation of unique, anatomy-inspired botanical illustrations that reflected the ingredients in their LDN CBD range. While these designs were a small part of the story, they exemplified our belief in blending creativity with purpose.  

This partnership was a testament to the power of collaboration and trust. While the company has since closed its doors, Not Bad Studio was proud to have played a role in supporting a brand that genuinely aimed to improve people’s lives. It was a relationship built on mutual respect, creativity, and a shared vision for doing good—both for their customers and the planet.
