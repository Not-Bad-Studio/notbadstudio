Questions

Why not just use an app?

There are plenty of apps that will animate a photo. Here's what you get from us that you don't get from them.

Can't I do this myself with an AI app?

You can, and for a quick post it might do. An app turns one photo into one clip and gives you whatever comes out. We pick the right photos, direct each shot, check every frame and cut it all into one film of one boat, property or room, with your brand on it.

How do you make sure nothing is added?

We check every shot frame by frame against the original photo. If anything appears, changes or moves that shouldn't, we redo the shot or hold that part of the frame to the photo. Names, numbers and logos stay sharp because we end each shot before they start to drift.

What about people in my photos?

We never animate people. We choose photos without them, and if someone is in frame we hold them still, so they only move with the boat or the camera. Silhouettes are fine.

Is it made with AI?

The motion comes from the same kind of tools the apps use. What you're paying for is everything around them: choosing the photos, directing each move, checking every frame, fixing what goes wrong and delivering a finished film in 24 hours.
